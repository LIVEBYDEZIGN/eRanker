The 1-star reviews, answered

Three concerns from 1-star reviews on Trustpilot. Answered by Ion, eRanker co-founder.

My conversion rate dropped.

Part of our process promotes your listing outside Etsy, on platforms like Facebook and Pinterest. That brings outside traffic. These visitors haven't searched for your product on Etsy, so you shouldn't expect them to buy like someone who has.

For Etsy, bringing in new people is a great deal: it hasn't paid to acquire them, and they can buy from your shop or other sellers. That's why I call outside promotion the best-case scenario for the marketplace.

Those visits still count in Etsy Stats. More visits without more orders lowers the percentage. But a visitor arriving from Facebook hasn't searched your keyword. Their visit doesn't measure how your product converts for that Etsy search.

Read Prashant Shah's 1-star review

Page 1 didn't bring more sales.

The first thing I check is the keyword. If hardly anyone searches for it, page 1 still brings few buyers. And if you already ranked there before, you've reached the same audience again.

A necklace can reach buyers through "pendant necklace", "gift for her" and "birthday gift for mom". One product, several searches. The idea is to reach people who weren't seeing it before.

Start with products that already sell, rank them for more relevant keywords and keep them there. If buyers see the product but don't choose it, we look at the photo, price and offer.

Read Amy Stockley's 1-star review

Did it hurt my visibility elsewhere?

For "gift for him", your listing competes with the other products on that page. Etsy looks at whether shoppers click and buy. If buyers keep choosing other products, your position can slip for that keyword.

That's why getting to page 1 is the start. You need to keep the ranking and give buyers a reason to choose your product: the photo, the price and the offer.

If sales fall, I check which listings and traffic sources changed. A lower overall conversion percentage alone doesn't show that Etsy penalised the whole shop.

Questions

How can every seller be on page 1?

Page 1 shows 48 organic listings for each keyword. Your test takes one of those spots, for the keyword you choose.

Can I get banned from Etsy?

We've run eRanker on our own Etsy shops since 2021, plus 200+ client shops since then. Zero flaggings so far. No passwords, no API keys, no access to your account.

Which listing should I test?

One that sold before and lost its spot. A listing that already sells is the fastest proof.

Is eRanker the same as eRank?

No. eRank shows you keyword data. eRanker moves your listing up for the keyword you choose. Different companies.
