Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What we collect

When you submit the form on this site, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, WhatsApp number, your Etsy listing URL, and your keyword choices. We use it for one purpose: to run the ranking test you requested and to communicate with you about it.

Analytics and cookies

This site uses Meta Pixel, Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, Reddit Pixel, Microsoft Clarity, and HubSpot to understand how visitors use the site and to measure advertising. These services may set cookies in your browser. You can block cookies in your browser settings at any time.

What we never do

We do not sell your data. We do not access your Etsy account, and we never ask for your Etsy password.
